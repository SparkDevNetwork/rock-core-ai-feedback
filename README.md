# Rock Core AI Feedback

> **This is a temporary repository.** It exists while we tune Rock's core AI skills and tools. Once things settle, we will archive it and move feedback back to the usual channels.

## Why This Repo Exists

Rock's core AI skills and tools are changing fast. We want your help to shape them while they are still in motion.

Most Rock feedback goes to one of two places:

- **[Core issues](https://github.com/SparkDevNetwork/Rock/issues)** for bugs and defects in shipped features.
- **[The Idea Board](https://community.rockrms.com/ideas)** for new feature requests.

Right now, AI feedback does not fit cleanly in either place. A skill can work as designed and still miss what you need. A small tweak to a tool description can change how well the AI uses it. These ideas move too quickly for the Idea Board and are not really bugs.

This repo is the space in between. It gives us a fast, open place to work with the community during this period of rapid change.

## What We Want to Hear

We are looking for two kinds of feedback.

### 1. Skills and Tools You Wish Rock Had

What can't you do with Rock's AI today? Tell us about the job you are trying to get done and where the AI comes up short. For example:

- An area of Rock that no skill covers yet.
- A task that takes too many steps, or that the AI can't finish at all.
- Data you can see in Rock but the AI can't reach.

### 2. Ways to Make the Existing Skills and Tools Better

Tell us where the current skills and tools fall short. For example:

- The AI picked the wrong tool, or did not use a tool when it should have.
- A tool returned too much data, too little data, or data in an unhelpful shape.
- A result was wrong, confusing, or slow.
- A tool needs a filter, option, or parameter that it does not have.

## How to Give Feedback

1. Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/SparkDevNetwork/rock-core-ai-feedback/issues) first. If someone already posted your idea, add a 👍 reaction and a comment with your use case.
2. If your idea is new, [open an issue](https://github.com/SparkDevNetwork/rock-core-ai-feedback/issues/new).
3. Keep each issue to one idea. This makes it easier to discuss and track.

Good feedback includes:

- **What you tried to do.** Describe the real ministry or admin task.
- **What happened.** Include the prompt you used and the AI's response, if you can.
- **What you expected.** Tell us what a good result looks like to you.
- **Your context.** Include your Rock version and the AI client you used (for example, Claude, ChatGPT, or Rock's built-in chat).

> **Warning:** This repo is public. Remove names, contact details, giving data, and other personal information from prompts and screenshots before you post them.

## What Happens to Your Feedback

The Rock core team reads the issues here and uses them to plan the next round of changes. Some ideas will become changes to the core skills and tools. Others may move to a core issue or to the Idea Board when that is the better home.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- **Bugs in Rock outside of AI.** Report these as a [core issue](https://github.com/SparkDevNetwork/Rock/issues).
- **General feature requests.** Post these on [the Idea Board](https://community.rockrms.com/ideas).
- **Security vulnerabilities.** Do not post these here. Report them privately through [Talos](https://security.rockrms.com/).

Thank you for helping us make Rock's AI better for every church.
